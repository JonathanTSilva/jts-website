I build reliable embedded and systems software — and lead the engineering work that gets complex products delivered.

I am a hands-on technical leader who turns ambiguous, high-risk work into clear plans, sound decisions, and dependable delivery. I contribute directly to architecture, code, integration, troubleshooting, and testing while aligning teams and improving engineering workflows.

My track record includes leading and executing the modernization of a five-year-old networking stack, resolving more than 20,000 merge conflicts while coordinating L2 and L3 domain teams. I have also driven test automation across more than 25 repositories, reducing manual test effort by more than 90%.

These outcomes reflect how I work: understand the system deeply, focus the team on the constraints that matter, and automate recurring manual work.

My foundation spans systems software, C/C++, Linux, RTOS, embedded systems, networking, and industrial systems. I am most effective where software meets hardware, protocols, real-time constraints, and long-lived products — but my contribution extends beyond any single technology or domain.

I hold an **M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering** from the **University of São Paulo (USP)**, with research in industrial cybersecurity. My work on industrial systems has been published in leading journals and presented at international conferences.

I value challenging engineering problems, strong technical teams, and international collaboration focused on building reliable products.
